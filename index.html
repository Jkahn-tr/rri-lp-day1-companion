The Seasons of Relationship
🌱 Spring

"Springtime in the beginning of everything — is it hard or easy to grow? Incredibly easy. If you start a business in springtime, you think you're a genius. No, you're just in the right season."

☀️ Summer

"What always follows spring is a testing time. Summer it's hot, it's a challenge. All of a sudden, the relationship doesn't feel effortless anymore."

🍂 Fall

"If you make it through the testing time of summer, the universe or God seems to give us this cool season called fall — a reaping time where all of a sudden everything has the most magnificent colors, where everything is easy, fruits available. It's all there."

❄️ Winter

"Winter is what makes us stronger. It bonds us the deepest part of our relationships — times where we came together when everything else looked like it was falling apart."

"There are seasons. And if you think everything's supposed to be springtime — you're going to be very frustrated and disappointed. But every season is designed by God for a higher purpose."
— Tony Robbins
The Generous Lover
"What would a totally generous lover do for the one that they adore, for their beloved? What would they do?"
— Tony Robbins

"If there's a theme of this program that will get you through all the seasons, it's you deciding that you are the source of the love in your relationship. As long as you're trying to get it from somebody, you're operating out of scarcity — and scarcity never feels good."


"What if that's all you did? You just decided the love that I'm going to experience is the love that I'm giving. I'm not going to worry about getting it or not."

"If you're sincerely generous in your love of this person and you're willing to give it openly and evenly, and you're not measuring what you're getting in return — it'll change everything."
— Tony Robbins
Beginner's Mind
"Start this week with a beginner's mind. A beginner's mind is open to anything. You don't have all the knowledge. You're curious. You're open. You're in awe. You want to learn."
— Tony Robbins

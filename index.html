How to Master Relationships: State and Meaning

There are only two things you really have to master to have an extraordinary, magnificent, and legendary relationship:

1
State
2
Meaning

Your state is the single most important factor in influencing the quality of your relationship. Everything is filtered through it. When two happy people get together, what do you have? A happy couple. What happens when two really unhappy people get together? You have a really unhappy couple.

There are two things that affect your state:

1   Physiology

2   Focus / Language

If you're going to be effective in any relationship, you have to understand what things mean. Being in a lousy state — from work, stress, finances, children, difficult people in your life — will block this, and it also comes from misinterpreting what your partner's intent was. You have to find the empowering meanings, rather than give things a disempowering meaning.

In order to master meaning, you have to understand three things:

1   The biochemical and physiological differences between women and men.

2   How we've been raised to interpret and use language differently based on gender.

3   An understanding of the individual Model of the World — desires, fears, goals, needs, rules — of your partner and of yourself.

The quality of your relationship is affected most powerfully by your state, not your partner's. If you want a great relationship, you must constantly put yourself and your partner in a great state.

The Seasons of Relationship

There are many seasons of our lives, seasons of relationship. The universe has set up the seasons in a very specific way — the seasons of nature, seasons of a human being's life, the seasons of a relationship.

Spring

Springtime in the beginning of everything — is it hard or easy to grow? Incredibly easy. If you start a business in springtime, you think you're a genius. No, you're just in the right season.

Summer

But then what always follows spring is a testing time. Summer — it's hot, it's a challenge. All of a sudden, the relationship doesn't feel effortless anymore. That happens to relationships too.

Fall

But if you make it through the testing time of summer, the universe or God seems to give us this cool season called fall. A reaping time where all of a sudden everything has the most magnificent colors, where everything is easy, fruits available. It's all there.

Winter

And when you think you're through it, of course, then winter comes. And if you don't understand that the seasons are coming, you're going to say "What's wrong with my relationship?" without understanding this is part of the process.

There are seasons. And if you think everything's supposed to be springtime — you're going to be very frustrated and disappointed. But every season is designed for a higher purpose.

You can accelerate the time that you spend in the greater seasons and spend a lot less time in the poorer, painful ones — but you can't avoid them completely. No one goes from springtime straight to fall. Some summers are hot, some are not so hot, some are long, some are short. Same thing with winters. But they're there.

Winter is what makes us stronger. Winter is what bonds us at the deepest part of our relationships. Times where we came together when everything else looked like it was falling apart — that bonded us deeper than anything.

The Generous Lover

If there's a theme of this program that will get you through all the seasons, it's you deciding that you are the source of the love in your relationship. As long as you're trying to get it from somebody, you're operating out of scarcity — and scarcity never feels good. Doesn't feel good to you, doesn't feel good to the other.

What would a totally generous lover do for the one that they adore, for their beloved? What would they do?

In the beginning, almost everybody's a generous lover. What do you want? Anything. Whatever you take, I'll do it, I'll make it happen. Then after seven days, seven weeks, seven months, seven years — somewhere between seven hours and seventy years — their rules change. And like, "No, I love you if you do this. If you don't, I'm pissed off at you."

But if you're a generous lover, the whole game changes. What if that's all you did? You just decided the love that I'm going to experience is the love that I'm giving. I'm not going to worry about getting it or not.

Two things will happen. If you're single, you won't stay single very long unless you really want to be — because generous lovers are very rare. If you're in a relationship, you'll reignite it completely. At first, it won't be trusted. But after a while, if you're sincerely generous in your love of this person — willing to give it openly and evenly, not measuring what you're getting in return — it'll change everything.
